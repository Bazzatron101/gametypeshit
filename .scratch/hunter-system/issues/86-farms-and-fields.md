**Blocked by:** 84: More building kinds (parent: 82)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Barns and farmhouses stand among the fields; a farming village has a windmill
- [ ] Fields keep off roads, water and steep ground, and the nature scatter keeps out of them (no trees in a wheat field)
- [ ] Fields can be walked through (crops are for looks); fences and buildings block like other footprints
